POS System Feature Roadmap

A structured 4-phase rollout plan, evolving from core POS operations into an enterprise-grade multi-branch restaurant management system.

Phase 1

MVP 1: Core POS

The foundation. Everything needed to open the doors, take orders, and process payments.

Auth & Setup

  • System Configuration & Versioning
  • Branch Settings (Tax Rates, Receipt Footer)
  • Role-Based Permissions
  • Staff User Accounts & Login
  • Multi-Currency Config (USD/KHR)

Menu Management

  • Product Categories
  • Food & Beverage Products
  • Modifier Groups (Sizes, Sugar Levels)
  • Modifier Options & Pricing

Operations & Ordering

  • Shift Management (Opening/Closing Cash)
  • Table Seating & Status
  • Order Flow (Dine-in, Takeaway)
  • Checkout (Cash, Card, QR)
  • Basic Receipt Printing
Phase 2

MVP 2: Kitchen & Inventory

Back-of-house operations. Routing tickets to chefs and automatically tracking ingredients.

Kitchen Display (KDS)

  • KDS Stations (Bar, Kitchen, Grill)
  • Digital Order Routing
  • Kitchen Printer Mapping
  • Order Timers & Status

Basic Inventory

  • Raw Ingredients & Units
  • Current Stock Levels
  • Recipes (Auto-deduction on sale)
  • Modifier Costing Recipes

Stock Adjustments

  • Manual Inventory Movements
  • Waste Records (Expired, Spilled)
  • Low Stock Threshold Alerts
Phase 3

MVP 3: Supply Chain

Procurement and logistics. Buying goods from suppliers and distributing them across branches.

Procurement

  • Supplier Directory
  • Purchase Orders (POs)
  • Goods Receipts (Verifying deliveries)
  • Purchase Returns

Accounts Payable

  • Supplier Payments tracking
  • Purchase History & Costing

Multi-Branch Logistics

  • Stock Transfers (Warehouse to Branch)
  • Transfer Status (In Transit, Received)
  • Multi-Branch Visibility
Phase 4

MVP 4: Growth & Analytics

Marketing, customer retention, and deep business analytics to scale the enterprise.

Loyalty & CRM

  • Membership Tiers (Bronze, Gold, VIP)
  • Loyalty Points (Earn & Redeem)
  • Customer Store Credit
  • Gift Cards Management

Marketing & Delivery

  • Promotions Engine (Buy X Get Y)
  • Discount Coupons
  • Delivery Integrations (Grab, FoodPanda)

Financial Analytics

  • Daily Sales Summaries
  • Expense Tracking & Categories
  • Product Profitability Reporting
  • Cash Flow Analysis
